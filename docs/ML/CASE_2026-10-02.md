# Case study: the 2 Oct 2026 BTC call spike, through every lens we have

**The trade:** C-BTC-87000-021026 (2 Oct daily expiry, settled 17:30 IST). MARK close about 4.9 at
03:00 IST, peak 321 in the 10:00 IST bar (**65x** from a 15m close; ~100x from the lowest prints).
BTC went 84.8k → 87.2k between 09:15 and 10:15 IST. Neighbours: 87500C 60x, 88000C 40x, 88500C
up to 147x from signal closes.

**It settled worthless.** BTC was 86,769 at 12:00 UTC, so every call ≥ 87,000 expired at ~0.1. The
multibagger existed only for a resting sell order on the spike. A target-at-25x rule (how the
buyer backtests score `y_25x`) captures it; any hold-to-expiry rule shows −100%.

| lens | what it said before 09:15 IST | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| his signals | 30 CALL events on this expiry fired 1 Oct 17:30 – 2 Oct 09:15. Those at 04:00–07:30 IST (30–90m, mostly otm_red_squeeze/red_squeeze, plus green_stairs 40m) peaked **68–147x** from their trigger close. 39 PUT events fired too (best 9x). | **identified the contracts** (and the wrong side as well) |
| R4 (agree with 4h trend) | 4h always-in = UP | take |
| **R5** (skip clean 20-day uptrends) | daily regime = clean uptrend | **VETOED EVERY ONE.** The A+ rule set would have missed it |
| wait-and-hold (3 candles) | only 18% of strike-signals held above the trigger low. Those that held still made ~47x (87500C 30m/40m; 88500C/89000C green_stairs 24–70x). The 90m signals' wait ended at 10:00, AFTER the move (1.1x) | kept some winners, dropped most; on 90m+ waiting missed it entirely, as the hold study predicts for higher TFs |
| quiet-before-the-storm picture | 1 Oct close: 7-day vol at the 14th percentile, weekend range 9th, price at 80th of its 7-day range | **MATCHED** (cuts ≤ 0.3 / ≤ 0.3 / ≥ 0.6). Rule = buy 3–6% OTM calls 3–8d out at 05:30 IST on 2 Oct |
| journal | entry 8 (30 Sep): wedge breaks with an explosion before the 2 Oct expiry, side unknown. Entry 10 (2 Oct 08:49, 26 min before): "bias for a breakout in coming days" | timing right; the bias pointed the right way this time |
| dashboard required move | a 1:100 at expiry needed spot 87,490 (+3.2%); the peak was 87,224 | at expiry: just missed. The "now" (with time value) column was the one that paid |
| forward test | live logging started 19:40 IST on 2 Oct | not running yet |
| 13:30 IST cliff, FOMC | not involved (09:15 IST move, no scheduled event) | — |

**Takeaways**
1. The signals pick the contracts. The squeeze signals on 30–60m charts were early (04:00–05:30 IST)
   and cheap (≈1–9).
2. R5 is the rule that cost this trade. One case doesn't overturn a rule tested on 142 weeks, but R5
   is about deep-OTM buying in clean uptrends, and this is precisely the case it gives up. Worth
   tracking in the forward log: how many R5-vetoed signals still hit 25x.
3. With these spikes the exit is the whole trade. A resting 25x sell order would have filled. Holding
   to expiry would have lost everything.
4. The quiet picture flagged the setup the evening before, from daily data alone.


## Follow-up: the 7 Oct breakdown, through the same lenses

BTC 85.5k → 83.5k (−2.3%) between 06:30 and 08:30 IST. ETH 2,696 → 2,590 (−3.9%). It broke the 1h rising support
from 18 Sep that he drew in the journal (entry 14).

| lens | what it said | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| his signals (forward-logged live, ≤3h after close) | PUT signals before the break: red_squeeze 240m P-84200 (7 Oct expiry) at 01:30 IST, **36x traded** so far; otm_wall 360m P-75000 (9 Oct) at 05:30, **23x traded**; several 6–12x | **caught it** |
| **R4** (4h trend must agree) | 4h always-in = UP, so every put was "skip" | **vetoed every winner**. It was R5 on 2 Oct (calls) and R4 on 7 Oct (puts): the trend filters veto the trades that come FROM trend breaks |
| level detector | 21-day support line breaks flagged at 00:30 and 04:30 IST (weakening). The 17-day line (his) + 3d + 8d lines broke at the 06:30 close, and a wedge break at 07:30 | right lines. The confirmation came after the first −1.3% leg, as the audit predicts |
| 1:100 grid, immediate expiry | put requirement jumped −2.4% → −15.3% → −32% at the break | **correct, and a finding.** The 7 Oct expiry had puts listed only down to 83,200 (about ±3% from listing). Once spot fell toward it, no cheap put existed, so after a sharp move the immediate expiry runs out of strikes on the side of the move and the NEXT expiry is the vehicle |
| forward test | 36 forward TAKE trades over 5 expiries: 0% traded 25x. Mark said 11%, because marks on near-worthless contracts sit below the tradeable minimum (e.g. mark 0.11 vs the lowest print 0.6) | too few to judge; it shows the mark-vs-trade gap live |

**Two weeks, two misses by the A+ filter, both on the side that paid.** It does not refute R4/R5 (142 weeks of
backing), but the forward log now measures it directly. **Track "R4/R5-vetoed signals that still reached 25x traded".**
