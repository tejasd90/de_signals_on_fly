## Conclusions that change

**Forward test (corrected).** 518 genuinely forward rows (was "1,688"). PICTURE-CALL 52 rows / 8 expiries: 0 at 25x
(implementable fills). All 14 forward 25x fills are PUTS in ONE episode (ETH/BTC 4–8 Oct crash, 6 contracts);
calls 0/164. With correct timing R4 read "down" on 8 Oct and three of them are now TAKE: TAKE 3/34, skip 11/260.
The earlier story "every crash put was skipped" was the timestamp bug. Puts in that crash paid ~10–12% whichever
rule; one episode, no conclusion. Backtest says skipping puts when the 4h always-in and regime read up is right on
average (1.06% vs 3.87% at 25x).

**Cross-sectional book.** +23%/yr, Sharpe 0.96, P=0.07 hedged; +13%/yr unhedged. Capacity with idle capital:
$10k +24%, $100k +19%, $1M +12%, $5M +6% (Sharpe 1.1–1.3, P 0.02–0.04, excludes 18 names with no contract value).
A marginal short-the-wild-coins effect, not a Tier-1 result.

**Setup 1 rests on one month.** 3,581 events: 25x 10.6%, 100x 5.1%. Three weeks of Aug 2026 carry 87% of the
100x hits. By year 100x: 2024 1.06%, 2025 0.85%, 2026 11.0%. Without those weeks: 25x 6.7%, 100x 2.4%; on 2024–25
rates alone 100x is about break-even. Week-block CI is too optimistic (3 consecutive weeks = 1 episode); month
blocks give P(EV<=0) 0.32. The thresholds were derived from the Aug-19 precursors. **Treat as in-sample, one
episode; only the forward log can confirm it.** Counting each traded fill once (events sharing a symbol and fill
candle are one trade) also cuts 2–20: 25x 8.6%, 100x 3.7%; <$2: 6.8% / 3.9% (was 10.3% / 7.1%), so <$2 is NOT
better (ASSUMPTIONS_AUDIT Decision 1 withdrawn).

**When not to trade is mostly the weekend.** Skip-30% edge: full model +24.4pp, Sat/Sun only +39.9pp, base vol +
weekend +41.8pp. Weekend "dead" rate 0.58 vs 0.22. Any day filter must now beat "skip weekends".

**Setup 3 (sell quiet straddles) only on Mon–Thu entries** (1–3d): quiet +0.351% vs rest −0.476%, P=0.000; Fri–Sun
quiet −0.054%, P=0.60. A post-hoc split of the same 145 days: candidate rule, forward only.

**Spike retention.** "Holding ≈ selling on average" was carried by 60 lottery contracts (0.1% of spikes = 25% of
hold value). Capping MARK peaks at 10x/20x/50x (above that is not fillable): hold minus sell −1.8 / −1.5 / −1.0,
every CI below 0. By time to expiry: <6h −1.03 [−1.71, −0.39], 6–24h −0.48 [−0.85, −0.06], 1–3d ≈0, >3d n.s.
**Booking at the first 3x is significantly better within 24h of expiry (69% of spikes)**; further out it is a
coin flip. The 60k spikes are ~8.7 distinct moments per expiry.

**New price.** Against the move in the opposite direction after the same event, a new close adds 1.5–2.6pp of
direction at most: it is a volatility flag only. The narrow-range cut with a past-only percentile gives the same
result (BTC 28% vs 36%, ETH 42% vs 60% for a ≥3% move).

**Channel-line puts "held-out 2026 P=0.043" is not held out**: the cell was picked from 64 with 2026 visible
(put-only maxT FWER p=0.71). ~4 weeks since 18 Sep are truly unseen.

**Trendline hover** re-run with the confirmation fix and repaired spot: unchanged (hover >12h → 57% break vs 65%;
support breaks continue 48%, reclaimed 66%).
