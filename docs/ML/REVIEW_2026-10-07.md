## Insights that connect studies

1. **Every survivor answers "whether / when", never "which way".**
   - the quiet-day filter has no direction (AUC 0.524);
   - his journal is 3/3 on absence calls and 0 on direction calls;
   - timing within an episode is weak (0.57);
   - the hurdle carry disappears once measured properly.

   The edges found are volatility-state edges. A direction edge has not been found anywhere.
2. **R5 and the quiet-before-the-storm picture point opposite ways on the same days.** R5 skips deep-OTM
   buying in clean 20-day uptrends. The picture (quiet + held up) is often exactly such an uptrend.
   2 Oct was both.
3. **The quiet-day filter is wrong on picture days.** On walk-forward days it flags 8 of 71 picture days
   as "skip", and those 8 had big moves on 12.5% of days, vs 1.5% on other skip days.
4. **The two seller filters don't stack.** See combination 3 below.
5. **The edge keeps showing up in the EXIT, not the entry.**
   - 2 Oct: 65x intraday, then expired worthless.
   - 25x-target scoring: holding to expiry loses what a target captures.
   - Mode 1: exiting at 3 DTE beats holding.
   - 7 Oct: the strikes ran out on the side of the move.

   Exits have been tested far less than entries.

## Combinations, ranked (preliminary)

1. **Quiet-before-the-storm picture × his CALL signals** (premium 2–20).
   - **Result:** 25x rate **11.1% vs 3.8%** on other days. Week-block bootstrap of the difference
     [+1.1, +15.2] pp, P = 0.006.
   - **By year:** 2024 11%, 2025 6.6%, 2026 16%.
   - **Puts on the same days:** 3.9%, i.e. nothing, which fits "all 6 hits were UP".
   - **Sample:** 3,604 events, but only 137 days, 46 weeks and about 10 effective episodes, concentrated
     in Aug 2026 and May 2025.
   - **Next:** episode-level leave-one-out, then forward logging.
2. **Lift R5 on picture days.** Calls in clean uptrends: 5.41% on picture days (n 804, 42 days) vs 2.07%
   otherwise. Carried by 3 days in May 2025, so this is a hypothesis only.
3. **Quiet-day seller filter × high-IV straddles is ANTI-additive** (1–3d straddle, Dec 2025 – Sep 2026):
   - quiet & high IV: −0.84% (n 37);
   - quiet & not high IV: +0.29% (n 249).

   Use the quiet filter only on non-high-IV days. Also, 00:00 UTC is the worst entry hour for 1–3d
   straddles; re-run `quiet_wf` at 08:00 UTC.
4. **Lift R4's veto after an opposing trendline break: REFUTED.** Against the 4h trend:
   - 25x rate 2.73% overall;
   - after an opposing 1h line break, 2.26% (worse; n 9,876 events, 141 weeks);
   - with a 4h break, 3.45%.

   Below the 4.33% break-even, so 7 Oct was the exception, not the rule. Caveat: least-squares lines.
   Re-check with the hull lines.
5. **Hurdles combined with anything: deprioritised**, because the base effect is not established.
6. **Sharp move in + long hug × hurdles: no.** The carry is the same (1.58% vs 1.57%, n = 25).
