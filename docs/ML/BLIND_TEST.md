Where the moves were: on A charts the best entry was a median **15 h before settlement** (40% within 12 h,
73% within 24 h). He stepped mostly on 1h (882 steps) and 2h (537); 15m was used once. Expiry-day moves
pass in a few 1h candles.

His calls were 34–168 h before expiry on 4 of 10, and the strike picked rarely moved (median 1.3x).
c015 (A) was called 3 h before expiry, after a 26x had already happened 28 h earlier.

What his notes say he looked for: "premiums decayed — no scope for parabolic rise", "curvy but no
conviction; need large up-moves relative to volatility", "price holding pattern, not parabolic",
"will need to come down and give swings". He said afterwards he "didn't feel many charts had parabolic setups".

Reading: the eye did not discriminate in this sample (0 hits; A no more called than C), and it did not flag
the late, fast expiry moves that make up group A. Consistent with the PREMIUM_BASE.md falsification.
Caveats: one sitting, n=10 calls; anonymisation removes context he may use live (asset, date, news,
the exact clock to settlement). Raw data stays in blindtest/data/ (git-ignored).
